Update ONLY the Olyxee main website landing/marketing page.

Do not redesign the full website.
Do not change unrelated product pages.
Do not rebuild the visual identity.
Do not change navigation unless necessary for the landing page.
Preserve existing functionality, responsiveness, and components where possible.

The goal is to reposition the landing page around:

- operational intelligence
- business speed
- growth
- fewer blockers
- less waiting
- less repetitive work
- faster execution
- Orgni as the flagship product
- Olyxee as the infrastructure company behind it

The landing page should feel modern, premium, enterprise-focused, minimal, and forward-looking.

Do not make the page sound like:
- a chatbot company
- a RAG company
- an enterprise search company
- a generic AI assistant company
- a knowledge-base company
- an AI agency
- a collection of random products

The page should tell one clear story:

Businesses grow.
As they grow, work becomes fragmented across people, systems, messages, documents, and departments.
That creates waiting, blockers, interruptions, and slow execution.
Olyxee is building operational intelligence infrastructure to reduce that friction.
Orgni is the flagship product that helps keep work moving.

Use this landing page narrative.

Headline:

“Built for businesses in motion.”

--------------------------------------------------

Create a clean interactive or animated product example.

Example command:

“@Orgni prepare everything for tomorrow’s client meeting.”

Then show Orgni completing work:

- Gathered recent emails
- Found the latest proposal
- Retrieved account history
- Identified unresolved issues
- Prepared the meeting brief
- Checked relevant calendars

Final state:

“Ready.”

The point of this section is to show that Orgni does not only answer questions.

Users can give Orgni work and receive a completed result.

Keep this section visually simple and believable.
